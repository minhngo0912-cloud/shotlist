Shot List

Move saved ideas, shot marks and notes to another device

On the device that has your marks, copy the code. Send it to your other device (for example with a message to yourself or the shared clipboard), paste it there and tap Import. Importing adds to what is already on the device; it never removes anything.

Subway photography is allowed by MTA rules without flash, tripods or lights. Diagrams are schematic. Place details were checked against published sources in October 2026; ideas and settings are starting points to test on the street. Saved and shot marks are kept in this browser on this device only.
